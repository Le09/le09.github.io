_Faking human music with computers (improved)._

Automated piano covers from any song? Basically, yes.

This is a follow-up to [my previous post on pop2piano]({% post_url 2023-03-03-POP %}).
The results back then were not too bad, but they only worked on a fairly narrow range of songs, and still required a lot of manual work to get something good.

[SheetSage2](https://map-yue2.github.io) is the audio-to-score transcription model released alongside the YuE2 music generation model.
It simply improves on everything, basically.

With SheetSage2, even the songs I had rejected came out at least quite good.
The range of music it handles seems essentially generalist.

## Same pipeline, new model

I reused the same pipeline as last time; [the whole pipeline is in this gist](https://gist.github.com/Le09/ed2559f2ae0dd58bbbaa55067a2eb5d6).
The only change was swapping pop2piano for SheetSage2, which conveniently already comes with a CLI.

It runs locally; the weights are on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/m-a-p/SheetSage2), under a non-commercial license.
There is no official UI, only a few community demos, but the command line is all you need:

```bash
python infer.py song.mp3 --output output --render-audio
```

This gives you an editable score (ABC), MIDI files for the melody and chords, and a piano rendering of the result.
All my tests, generative and transcription, ran in under 2 minutes, so it's a surprisingly efficient model.

## Generating songs

The project can also generate songs from prompts with YuE2, and you can steer the generation by feeding it a score, such as the ones SheetSage2 produces.
